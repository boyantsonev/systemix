## Rationale

Decisions queue here instead of shipping silently. The queue is the visible
form of the autonomy dial: at every trust tier, self-modification and
experiment closes arrive as cards a human resolves — the engine proposes,
humans close. The amber border on a pending card is the "a human owes this a
call" signal; resolved cards dim rather than disappear so the ledger stays
legible on the same surface.

## States

- **pending** — amber border, action buttons (approve / reject / defer).
- **resolved** — dimmed, read-only; the decision and resolver are recorded and
  the card gains a permalink in the decisions ledger at `/contract/decisions`.

## Drift

None recorded. Card types (close-proposal, drift-resolution,
instrumentation-approval, new-token, engagement-snapshot) are defined next to
the component; adding a type without a renderer is the drift to watch for.
