## Rationale

0.5rem is the single radius the whole shadcn surface derives from — cards,
inputs, and sheets compute their corner scale off this one token. It sits
between "sharp enough to read as a tool" and "soft enough for a landing page",
which matters because Systemix renders both from the same system.

## Drift

None recorded. Rounded-full pills (nav, chips) are intentional exceptions,
not drift — they opt out of the scale by design.
