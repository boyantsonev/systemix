This page renders the actual `Button`, `Input`, and `Select` components from
`src/components/ui/`, not screenshots or a mockup — it can't drift from the code.

Two filled button colors: `primary` (white/cream — borrows `--foreground`/`--background`,
so it inverts automatically across light and dark) and `secondary` (amber — the brand
color, `--primary`/`--primary-foreground`). `outline`, `ghost`, and `link` cover
lower-emphasis actions; `destructive` is for irreversible ones.
