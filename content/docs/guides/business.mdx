## What Systemix does for you

Your design system gets an **engineered memory**: every component, token, and
product bet is a file in your repo with its rationale and its evidence
attached. When you ask "why does the landing look like this?" or "didn't we
already test that?", the answer is written down — with a date, a confidence,
and a link to the experiment that earned it.

On top of that memory runs a **weekly learning loop**: a scheduled runner pulls
your signals (PostHog first; Figma, repo, Linear, sprint data as they're
wired), evaluates your running experiments, and queues decisions for you.
You read one brief and make the calls; the system writes them back so the next
sprint starts from what the last one proved.

## Your 10-minute start

1. **See it live.** This site is a Systemix instance — open
   [/experiments](/experiments) to watch the real loop, and
   [/contract/memory](/contract/memory) to see the memory it earns.
2. **Pick your rung.** The open-source kit is free and self-serve. From there
   the ladder is simple: the [AI-Readiness Report](/audit) ($149) scores your
   repo, the [AI Kit](/kit) ($299) sets you up to run the loop, and the sprint
   (from $2,500) is [Boyan wiring the loop to your stack](mailto:boyan.works@gmail.com?subject=Systemix%20scoping%20call) —
   brand cloned in session one, experiments live by end of week.
3. **Frame your first bet.** One sentence: *who* (ICP), *what they're trying to
   do* (JTBD), *what you believe* (hypothesis), *what number moves* (metric).
   That sentence becomes `experiments/<id>.mdx` — the unit of evidence.

## Your weekly loop

- The runner sweeps running experiments daily and queues **close proposals**
  when evidence clears your thresholds. It never closes — you do.
- Once a week, review the queue (the Home dashboard) and the
  [memory feed](/contract/memory): what was learned, what expired, what to bet
  on next.
- Every decision you make becomes a cited line in `LEARNINGS.md` — the
  compounding asset.
