# Example goal

Humans give goals — the engine never self-assigns one. State the outcome you want
(a KPI or business result) and how you'll know it's met, and who it's for (`icp`).
`success-criteria` and `kill-if` are read by a human (or Hermes) when deciding
whether to keep pursuing it — nothing here is machine-enforced yet.

Experiments in `experiments/` link to this goal via their own `goal:` field
(`systemix experiment new <id> --goal example-goal`, or the `/init-experiment`
skill) — nothing needs to be hand-maintained on the goal file itself; the link
and the experiment count are both derived live from the experiment side.
