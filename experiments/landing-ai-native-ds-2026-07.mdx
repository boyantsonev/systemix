## Why this hypothesis

v7 made the abstract loop the pitch and demoted the design system to an optional
substrate — and the landing became a burden to understand. The founder's read:
the design system is not the substrate, it is **the flagship use case** of the
loop, and the consultancy upsell. There are many AI-slopped design systems;
Systemix is the AI-native one — MDX files as the decision layer, a learning loop
for the design rationale behind every component, custom skills that update
around the user's own workflow, living in Claude. The loop serves both verticals
(design system + experiments); the hero should lead with the concrete one people
recognize.

## Given

A pre-PMF founder (or anyone who owns a design system) lands on Systemix from a
feed and gives the hero five seconds. Today they meet "Your shipping memory" —
an abstraction they must decode before they know what Systemix is for.

## The AI workflow

The repositioning bet → Hermes frames the hypothesis (ICP · JTBD · metric) →
write the variant copy (hero + the services ladder) → ship at 100% through the
existing `landing-hero` variant seam → measure `hero_cta_click` per visitor,
before vs after → a human decides → the learning is written back. Ship-and-compare,
not a split: at ~6 visitors/30 days a real A/B cannot resolve, so the honest
read is directional.

## Measurement notes

- `hero_cta_click` previously fired only on the GitHub link; the ship extends it
  to every hero CTA (`cta: github | install`). The before-window baseline for
  the install CTA is approximated by the pre-existing `install_command_copied`
  event.
- `book_a_call` stays experiment #1's site-wide signal; the new design-partner
  CTA fires it with `location: services-design-partner`, so the ladder's effect
  is visible without contaminating #1's metric.
- The full-page body re-anchor (problem/effect/loop/CTA/metadata repositioned to
  the design-system framing) ships in the same PR and evidence window — the
  variant is the repositioned page as a whole; the hero metric still owns the
  read.

## Conclusion

The design-system-first hero engages a meaningfully higher share of visitors
than the loop-abstraction hero, and the consultancy ladder produces its first
design-partner inquiry.

## Decision criteria

- `promote`: hero CTA clicks per visitor rise meaningfully over the prior window
  → the design-system framing becomes the landing's spine (extend it to
  Problem/Effect/Loop in a follow-up pass).
- `iterate`: engagement flat but design-partner inquiries arrive → the offer
  works, the hero words don't; tighten the H1/body, keep the ladder.
- `kill`: no lift and no inquiries → the category framing isn't the hook;
  revisit which vertical leads (experiments-first?) or the ICP.
